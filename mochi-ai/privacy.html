Privacy Policy

Mochi AI · Effective November 17, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes what the Mochi AI developer app and this website collect and how they use it. Mochi AI is the app behind the TikTok account @mochi.knows.ai. Contact: mochi.knows.ai@gmail.com.

2. Data the app accesses

Through TikTok’s Login Kit, the app accesses basic profile information (user.info.basic) of the connected @mochi.knows.ai account, only to link that account. Through TikTok’s Content Posting API, it uploads video files and captions (video.publish and video.upload) to that same account. The app does not access followers, messages, or any other TikTok account.

3. Data the app stores

The app stores the TikTok access token and refresh token for the connected @mochi.knows.ai account, kept securely and used only to publish videos to that account. Tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok’s settings, which immediately stops the app’s access.

4. Data this website collects

This website is a static set of pages. It collects nothing: no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect data from viewers of our videos, we do not track visitors, we run no advertising, and we do not build profiles of anyone.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone. The only data sent to a third party is the video and caption content we publish to TikTok itself, and the basic profile information TikTok returns when the account is connected.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the @mochi.knows.ai account stays connected to the app. To have stored tokens deleted, revoke access in TikTok’s settings or email mochi.knows.ai@gmail.com and we will delete them.

8. Children

Mochi AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: mochi.knows.ai@gmail.com.
